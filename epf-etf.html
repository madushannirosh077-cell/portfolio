EMPLOYEE SOCIAL SECURITY

EPF & ETF

Nirosh Company Team recognises the importance of employee social security and maintains applicable EPF and ETF contribution arrangements for eligible employees in accordance with Sri Lankan requirements.

🏦
EMPLOYEES' PROVIDENT FUND

E.P.F

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a mandatory retirement and social security contribution scheme applicable to covered employees in Sri Lanka.

8% Employee Contribution
12% Employer Contribution
Total Minimum Contribution: 20%

The minimum EPF contribution is calculated according to applicable Sri Lankan EPF requirements.

🤝
EMPLOYEES' TRUST FUND

E.T.F

The Employees' Trust Fund (ETF) provides an employer-funded social security benefit for eligible employees in Sri Lanka.

3% Employer Contribution
Employer-Funded Benefit

ETF contributions are made by the employer and should not be deducted from the employee's monthly earnings.
